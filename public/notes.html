How a note is built

Every entry stacks in three, so you can stop wherever you like and never feel you quit early. The line is one sentence, plain. The note explains what the evidence is and how far it can be pushed. The record is the citation and where it sits — the manuscript, the edition, the page — so you can put your own hands on it.

A museum sets three things beside a painting: a title card, the wall text, and the accession number. Same object, three doors, and nobody is embarrassed at any of them.

The Word Studies

Not tied to one episode. A word, what it carried, what it carries now, and every place you can check it. These are the sheets the episodes are built on.

BR-W-001
Belief  ·  Believe
One is a noun and one is a verb, and almost everything turns on which one you were handed. Emunah is steadiness; he’emin is putting your weight on something. A floor that is never stood on is not known to hold.
In preparation
BR-W-002
πίστις  ·  pistis
In the courts and the counting houses it meant proof — a guarantee you could produce. It did not mean believing without evidence. That came later, and it cost us the word.
In preparation
BR-W-003
רָחַף  ·  rachaph
To hover; to brood, of a bird over the nest. Three occurrences in the whole Hebrew Bible, one of them disputed — which leaves two, and one of the two is the water before the world.
