What this tool does, what it costs, and where it stops
Terms & Limits
Plain Language  ·  No Surprises Read Before You Rely On It BR-TL-001  · 

A contract nobody reads is not consent, it is paperwork. So this one is written to be read in four minutes, in the words I would use if you asked me across a table.

Abstract

This page sets out what the Recovery Engine is for, how many searches are free, what happens when you pass that number, what it costs if you choose to continue, what is kept about your use of it, and — most importantly — what the tool can and cannot be trusted to do. Nothing here is hidden in a longer document somewhere else. This is the whole of it.

One
What this is for
A study aid, offered to a person who intends to check it.

The Recovery Engine returns the original-language text behind an English verse, what a term carried before centuries of translation moved it, and the passages where that movement changes the reading. It is built for the person who wants to go and look for themselves.

It is a tool, not an authority. It does not replace study, a teacher, a lexicon, or your own reading. Where the manuscripts disagree, it should tell you they disagree. Where a conclusion on this site is mine rather than the field's, it is marked as a reading — How This Work Is Done explains that distinction and the method behind it.

Use what it gives you the way a tradesman uses a table of values: as a starting figure from someone who did the arithmetic, checked against the job in front of you before you sign anything.

Two
What it costs
Free until you have used it enough to know whether it is worth paying for.

Eight searches a month, free

No account needed, no card, no sign-up wall. The allowance renews each month. Most people who come from an episode, look something up, and leave satisfied will never reach the end of it.

Nothing is shown to you about payment until you reach that number

There is no price on the search page and no countdown ticking at you while you read. If you pass eight searches in a month, that is the first you will hear of it — because that is the first point at which it concerns you.

If you want to continue — Study, $5 a month

Unlimited searches, early access to new terms, and the newsletter. Cancel whenever you like; it stops at the end of the period you have paid for.

If you want it on your own site — Embed, $15 a month

Unlimited searches for you, 500 embedded searches a month for your site, an API key, and a usage dashboard. Built for churches and ministries who want the tool in front of their own people.

Payments are handled by Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe's own page and are never seen by, sent to, or stored on this site. If a charge ever looks wrong, write to me before you dispute it and I will sort it out.

Three
What is kept
Less than you would expect, and here is the use of each piece.

Your searches

The words you type are recorded so I can see what people are actually asking. That record shapes what gets built next and which terms get written up first — the questions people bring are the best map of where the teaching has gone thin.

Your count

How many searches you have used this month is kept in your own browser. Clear your browser data and it goes with it. If you create an account, the count follows you across devices instead.

Your account, only if you make one

An account stores your name, your email, and your subscription state. You do not need one to search. Ask and I will delete it.

Nothing here is sold, and there is no advertising on this site. The whole point of the work is that you can check it — an arrangement that quietly collects and resells the people checking would be a contradiction.

Four
Where it stops
The honest boundary, stated before you need it.

What you should not do with this

Do not treat a single search as settled fact. Follow it to the source. That is what the references are for, and it is the habit this whole site exists to build.

The Recovery Engine is offered as it is, and it can be wrong — a reference mistyped, a term with a contested meaning, an answer drawn from a repository entry that needs revising. If you find one, tell me and I will fix it and say that I did.

It is not legal, medical, financial, or pastoral advice, and it is not a substitute for a person who knows you.

Availability. This runs on ordinary hosting and depends on services I do not control. It will occasionally be down. If you are paying and it is down for any meaningful stretch, write to me and I will make it right.

Changes. If these terms change, the date at the top of this page changes with them, and anything that affects what you pay will be said plainly rather than absorbed into a longer document.

Conclusion
The short version
If you read nothing else.

Eight free searches a month. No card, no account, no price shown until you actually reach the limit. Five dollars a month if you want to keep going, cancel any time. Your searches are recorded so the work gets better; nothing is sold. And every answer is a starting point you are expected to go and check.
